What's Included

8 steps, one 90-minute session.

Every step builds on the last — working deeper with each stage to deliver results you can actually see and feel.

01

Deep Back Cleanse

A thorough foam cleanse across the shoulders, back and waist — removing daily buildup, excess oil and impurities. Refreshing and grounding from the very first step.

02

Ice Serum & Steam

A cooling serum is applied followed by warm steam — opening every pore across the back and softening the skin's surface so that subsequent steps can penetrate more effectively.

03

Deep Extraction

Thorough extraction of congestion, oil and skin debris across the entire back. The skin is left feeling unclogged, breathing freely and genuinely lighter.

04

Organic Back Scrub

An organic scrub with fine, gentle texture is massaged across the full back — exfoliating again at a deeper level, removing dullness and revealing smoother, more even-toned skin underneath.

05

Acid Peel & Oil Prep

A light acid application to clear any remaining dead skin cells, followed by essential oil to prepare the skin for maximum absorption in the massage step ahead.

06

Essential Oil Massage

A 20-minute professional shoulder and back massage using a brightening essential oil blend. Targeted techniques stimulate meridian pathways, improve circulation and lock in the treatment's active ingredients.

07

Japanese Hokkaido Whitening Mask

A Japanese Hokkaido whitening body mask is applied and wrapped across the back — delivering deep hydration and brightening actives at a cellular level. The result: skin that looks visibly clearer and more luminous.

08

Shoulder & Neck Relaxation

A final shoulder, neck and back relaxation to close out the full treatment — easing any remaining tension and leaving you feeling genuinely lighter from head to waist.

Before you book
