What's Included

9 steps, one 90-minute session.

Every step is performed in sequence — each one building on the last.

01

Deep Cleanse

A thorough double cleanse to remove makeup, sunscreen, and daily buildup — giving the skin a clean, receptive base for everything that follows.

02

Exfoliation

Gentle exfoliation to remove dead skin cells, smooth texture, and allow deeper product absorption throughout the rest of the treatment.

03

Eyebrow Trimming

A light tidy of the brow area to frame the face — included as part of the full facial experience.

04

Full Extraction

Careful manual extraction of congestion and blocked pores. Our therapist works methodically to clear without aggravating the skin.

05

Cool Crystal

A cooling and calming step applied after extraction to soothe the skin, reduce redness, and minimise the appearance of pores.

06

Relaxing Facial Massage

A deeply relaxing massage using targeted techniques to improve circulation, ease facial tension, and encourage a natural glow.

07

Shoulder Massage

A short shoulder and neck massage to help you fully unwind — because good skin starts with relaxation.

08

Mask (Cream / Powder)

A treatment mask selected for your skin type — hydrating, brightening or calming — to lock in the benefits of the session.

09

Finishing Skincare

A complete finishing routine including toner, serum and moisturiser — leaving your skin balanced, nourished and ready to go.

Come with a clean or lightly made-up face if possible. Avoid heavy retinol or active treatments in the 24–48 hours before. Arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in without rushing — the therapist will consult with you before starting.
